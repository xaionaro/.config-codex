# Worked examples and source adaptations

## Retry outage: local effect versus recovery

Supplied records show an omitted-jitter replay reduced peak retries from 5x to 1.5x after a patch. Production recovery followed both upstream throttling and rollback; gateway telemetry has a gap. The coordinator dispatches one evidence investigator and an independent critic with the same records and read-only authority. They preserve the omitted jitter as a demonstrated local mechanism while leaving its production contribution and mitigation interaction uncertain.

The critic discovers that the “updated” runbook still requires a dead primary and that a prior “fix retries” item closed after adding only a dashboard. Both enter the existing incident register. The payment owner accepts the replay followup; platform action ownership remains pending while its team is unavailable. No external tickets or assignments are invented.

| Finding | Disposition and proof |
| --- | --- |
| Retry synchronization | Patch implemented; local peak reduced. End-to-end failover effect pending a representative authorized replay. |
| Late alert | Threshold changed; effect pending a fixture showing detection within the agreed impact target. |
| Runbook dead end | Document observed safe secondary route; validate with primary unavailable before claiming recovery guidance works. Platform acceptance pending. |
| Prior followup ineffective | Link previous item; investigate whether symptoms share a mechanism rather than asserting recurrence. Change closure criteria to measure the promised retry effect. |
| Causal evidence gap | Preserve dissent; isolate throttling and library behavior in a disposable test when available. |

Recovery is observed. Investigation can receive a bounded disposition after independent challenge with uncertainty retained. Followups remain incomplete: missing runtime and unaccepted platform work cannot be converted into success by a deadline. The maintained replay, alert fixture and exercised runbook become durable learning when implemented and verified.

## Organizational mechanism: successful rescues become assurance

Illustrative software records: nine overloads ended after manual throttling without customer errors. Approved page thresholds moved from 10 to 30 to 90 seconds to reduce noise; the assurance note cited those rescues without testing capacity or operator availability. Before a later outage, an engineer's conditional warning and request for a two-region test became “under investigation,” then “no known blocking issues” in the director's packet. The test did not run; the engineer did not know the packet had changed. The ordinary reviewer shared the launch target. These records support specific assurance and information failures, not a diagnosis of the whole organization or intentional suppression.

The coordinator sends the original warning, summaries, approvals, threshold history and successful interventions to actual independent investigators. The critic can inspect raw adverse records and challenge scope and closure without responsibility for meeting the launch target. Another signature alone does not establish that function; no new standing board or release veto follows.

| Supported mechanism | Existing register action and observable effect |
| --- | --- |
| Prior rescues substituted for explaining degraded capacity | Link the near misses and changed acceptance criteria. Test bounded retries, useful throughput and capacity under representative bursts, including an unavailable/busy usual operator. Do not blindly restore 10 seconds: compare detection needs with legitimate noise and workload costs. |
| Warning uncertainty and unrun test disappeared before approval | Trace raised → routed/changed → received → decision/disposition → feedback using contemporaneous records. Change the maintained decision packet/routing so material uncertainty, unperformed tests and dissent reach the actual decision owner. Replay a conditional warning through it and inspect that owner's received packet and recorded response/feedback; an archived appendix or “speak up” training alone cannot prove repair. |
| Review shared a delivery goal; author lacked feedback | Investigate what this changed in the actual decision, staffing and authority. Verify that a qualified challenge can inspect raw evidence and retain a contrary conclusion. Do not infer bias from reporting lines alone or promise real people's behavior from a document replay. |
| Useful manual recovery depended on personal knowledge | Preserve the rescue, move the validated path to maintained guidance and exercise recognition, access and competing duties. An uncued mixed-alert drill tests diagnosis/prioritization; giving the fault and a preauthenticated terminal tests only command execution. |
| Previous corrective work was marked Done | Compare its promised effect with recurrence evidence and actual work. Repair the maintained control and test that effect; publication, attendance and another policy statement are activities. |

Keep actions owned and authorized through the existing workflow. Unavailable tests, unaccepted service ownership and real organizational adoption remain pending; a successful local packet replay is bounded proof, not “culture fixed.” Recovery, investigation disposition and followup completion remain separate.

**Boundary:** an exception supported by representative tests, authorized risk rationale and reconsideration conditions can be justified. A warning may be technically rejected after its uncertainty reaches the decision owner and a discriminating test addresses it; preserve the evidence and feedback without inventing silencing. Investigate the connected record, not every waiver or the entire organization.

## Origins and limits

These are source-specific adaptations, not uniform traits of Boeing/NASA or imported organizational requirements:

| Primary source | Supported lesson and software adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| [Rogers Commission, Appendix F](https://www.nasa.gov/history/rogersrep/v2appf.htm), F1–F2, F4 | Feynman's observations describe weakening acceptance criteria and unexplained erosion treated as safe after prior flights. Examine anomalies, waivers and manual rescues against intended controls. The appendix also describes successful software verification; prior success and tested exceptions need their actual evidentiary scope. |
| [Rogers Chapter V](https://www.nasa.gov/history/rogersrep/v1ch5.htm), p. 82, and [Chapter VII](https://www.nasa.gov/history/rogersrep/v1ch7.htm), pp. 152–153 | Incomplete information bypassed key decisionmakers; safety staffing, reporting and independence had specific weaknesses. Reconstruct warning transformation and actual knowledge; assess functional challenge rather than copying an organization chart. |
| [Columbia Accident Investigation Board, Volume I](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/columbia/reports/CAIBreportv1.pdf), pp. 9, 177 | Identifies schedule/resource context, reliance on past success, communication barriers and constrained differences of opinion. Ask which mechanisms affected this incident. A deadline alone proves no causal finding. |
| [NTSB ASR-19-01](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/ASR1901.pdf), pp. 1, 7–10 | Questions assumed pilot recognition/response under multiple alerts in Boeing 737 MAX assessments. Test actual cues, access, prioritization and concurrent demands. Recommendations arose during ongoing investigations; this is neither a complete Boeing-culture verdict nor accident-pilot blame. |
| [NTSB AIR-25-04](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/AIR2504.pdf), sections 2.3–2.5, A-25-32/33 | Examines procedural usability, workforce/change conditions, persistent nonconformance and corrective-action effectiveness in the Flight 1282 door-plug investigation. Examine why prior actions failed and verify changed work. Its recommendation for a Boeing culture review grants no software-organization audit authority. |
| [NTSB investigative process](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/process/Pages/default.aspx) | Distinguish evidence gathering, analysis, report acceptance and recommendation follow-through. This skill grants no aviation/regulatory authority. |
| [Google emergency response](https://sre.google/sre-book/emergency-response/) and [lessons from other industries](https://sre.google/sre-book/lessons-learned/) | Disaster and Recovery Testing (DiRT), hidden dependencies and recovery exercises. Choose the cheapest faithful authorized replay; no compulsory disruptive production drill. |
| [Google postmortem culture](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/) | Accountable ownership, tracked actions and verifiable end states; prefer system/process improvements over blame. |
| [Meta practitioner account](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/meta-production-environment/) | Independent senior review and cause-related followup checks described by a practitioner; not a universal/current Meta process specification. |
