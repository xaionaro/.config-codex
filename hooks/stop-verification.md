# Stop Verification

Use the smallest evidence that answers the user’s actual request. Verification is useful engineering practice, but ordinary completion does not require a receipt, hash, commit, role, workflow artifact, or exact document grammar.

## Concrete Stop Boundaries

A valid direct active marker for the current session and canonical working directory receives one reminder to resume active work or complete normal teardown. An unchanged later callback continues. A malformed or scope-mismatched direct marker, or a resolved destructive/broad/cross-session target, may be paused only long enough to identify and correct that concrete target.

ATE state, peer markers, callback metadata, transcripts, historical review notes, ledger state, and checklist formatting are advisory. They cannot manufacture ownership or hold ordinary work.

## Practical Verification

1. Inspect the changed behavior or answer against the request.
2. Run a focused check when it can falsify a material claim.
3. Preserve unrelated work and avoid exposing credentials.
4. Report actual blockers and residual risks without inventing ceremony.
